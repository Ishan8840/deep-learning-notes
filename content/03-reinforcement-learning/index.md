The path runs from the agent–environment loop to policy updates that use estimated advantages.

1. [Markov decision processes](01-markov-decision-process/index.md) — states, actions, transitions, and rewards.
2. [Generalized advantage estimation](05-generalized-advantage-estimation/index.md) — balancing bias and variance.
3. [Proximal policy optimization](06-ppo/index.md) — clipped on-policy updates.
4. [Soft Actor-Critic](07-soft-actor-critic/index.md) — off-policy learning with entropy.

For hands-on examples, see [tabular methods](https://github.com/Ishan8840/deep-learning/tree/main/03-reinforcement-learning/01-tabular-methods), [DQN](https://github.com/Ishan8840/deep-learning/tree/main/03-reinforcement-learning/02-deep-q-learning), and [policy gradients](https://github.com/Ishan8840/deep-learning/tree/main/03-reinforcement-learning/03-policy-gradients).
