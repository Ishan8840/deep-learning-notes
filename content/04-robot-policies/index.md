These methods predict a chunk of future actions from images and robot state. The [implementation repository](https://github.com/Ishan8840/deep-learning/tree/main/04-robot-policies) contains ACT, diffusion-policy, and flow-matching-policy examples.

1. [Action chunking with transformers](01-act/index.md) — predict a chunk directly.
2. [Diffusion policy](02-diffusion-policy/index.md) — denoise an action sequence.
3. [ALOHA Unleashed](03-aloha-unleashed/index.md) — scaling diffusion-based robot policies.
4. [VITA Flow Matching](04-vita-flow-matching/index.md) — a flow-based policy design.
5. [A2A Flow Matching](05-a2a-flow-matching/index.md) — another route to action generation.

The related [flow-matching fundamentals](../02-generative-models/05-flow-matching/index.md) explain the vector-field objective behind these policies.
